### Deen ka Maqsad aur Quran ka Evaluation
Azeez doston, hum aapke samne suraton ka **takeaways** pesh kar rahe hain taake aap ek taweel safar ke baad dekhein ke humne haasil kya kiya, zindagi mein kya aaya aur kya chhoot gaya. Yaqeen maniye, jo chhoot gaya ho ya jo aaya ho, yeh sab Allah ke samne pesh hona hai. Yeh Quran ka ek ek lafz woh hai jo meri aur aapki zindagi ke andar laana hai. Kiyonki kal Qayamat ke din jo hisaab hum kehte hain, woh hisaab isi Quran ke mutabiq hoga. Agar ismein humari koi cheez chhoot gayi hai toh kal Allah ke paas humein iski pakad bhi hai, aur jo cheez humari zindagi mein aa gayi hai uske hisaab se humara inaam bhi hai.

Quran aaya hi isiliye ke iske ek ek lafz pe humari zindagi ke andar fikr baney, soch baney aur hum waise insaan ban jayein jaise insaan Allah ko matloob hai. Allah Ta'ala hum sabko waise insaan banne ki taufeeq ata farmaye. Waise insaan humein banate chal, kal Allah ke saath rehne ke hum qaabil ban jayein.

Toh doston, hum aapke samne **Paanch Nabvi ki Fikri Tarjeehat** pesh kar rahe thay, **Aakhirat ki Tarjeehat** pesh kar rahe thay. Aakhirat ki priorities kya honi chahiye jab aapki meri zindagi mein aakhirat aa chuki ho? Jiska hum aqeeda rakhte hain, uski preparation kya hai? Uske liye humari aur aapki zindagi ke andar priorities kya bani hai? Pehle paanch priorities aapke samne laayi gayi thi aur baaki ke paanch second part aaj Insha'Allah hum pesh karne ki koshish karenge.

Lekin doston, sunana maqsood nahi hai, batana maqsood nahi hai, ilm dena maqsood nahi hai. Balki maqsad yeh hai ke ab tak humne jitna sab padha hai, hum sab milkar jis ke oopar mehnat ki hai, task complete kiye hain, tafseer padhi hai, shaan-e-nuzool dekha hai, zamane nuzool dekha hai, toh waqt yeh hai ke humari zindagi mein kya cheez aa rahi hai aur kya chhoot rahi hai. Yeh **evaluation** ke liye humari classes chal rahi hain, yeh revision chal raha hai, aur ek hum **to-do list** banayein taake zindagi ke andar woh aa sakey.

Madani daur ke andar jab Sahaba gaye hain toh zindagi ke andar Aakhirat aa chuki thi. Aakhirat unke liye priority ban chuki thi, Allah ki mohabbat unke dil ke andar ghar kar chuki thi, har cheez Allah ke liye qurban karne ka jazba unke andar aa chuka tha. Toh Madani daur ke andar jab Rasulullah (ﷺ) gaye, Sahaba ne Madani muashira bana sake aur phir poori duniya ke andar deen ko la sakey. Lekin khud apne ghar ke, dil ke ghar ke andar Allah ki mohabbat ghalib na aaye, Aakhirat humari priority na baney, aur zindagi ke andar maqsad ki clarity na aaye toh phir Madani daur mein jaana koi faydemand nahi.

Toh isiliye aapke samne yeh pesh kiya ja raha hai taake aap dekhein ke kya chhoot gaya hai aur kya hum aur behtar apne andar la sakte hain. Hum apne saathiyon ko phir se Madani daur mein aane se pehle call karenge, unse baat karenge, taake ek naye level se aur naye jazbe ke saath hum Madani daur mein jayein. Hum is Quran ke andar doobenge aur apne aap ko sanwarenge, apne ignorance ko knowledge mein tabdeel kar denge. Isi niyat ke saath hum aaj aage badhenge.

### Guzashta Peshkash: Pehli Paanch Aakhirat ki Tarjeehat (Priority 1-5)
Doston, hum aapke samne ab tak **Paanch Nabvi ke Marahle** mein Aakhirat ki tayyari aur zindagi ki correct tarjeehat ki paanch aaine dikha chuke hain.

1.  **Surah Takasur (Priority 1): Hirs aur Tama ko Chhorna, Maut ko Yaad Rakhna**
    *   Jab dil duniya ki andhi daud mein fans jata hai toh qabr yaad nahi rehti, hisaab yaad nahi rehta. Apni masrufiyat mein insaan apni maut aur apni laash ko bhool jata hai. Is andhi daud se nikalna hai.

2.  **Surah Ma'un (Priority 2): Imaan ko Amal se Zahir Karna**
    *   Sirf imaan ka dawa kaafi nahi. Namaz, ikhlaq, yateemon ke huqooq, miskeen ke saath narmi aur chhoti chhoti madad ko apni zindagi mein laana zaroori hai.

3.  **Surah Shams (Priority 3): Dil aur Amal ki Safai (Tazkiya)**
    *   Asal kaamyabi fikr ki paaki aur amal mein ikhlas hai. Jab tak amal riya, laparwahi aur gunah se na bachey, insaan haqeeqi falah tak nahi pahunch sakta.

4.  **Surah Buruj (Priority 4): Imaan ki Hifazat par Sabit Qadam Rehna**
    *   Jab duniya mein challenges, nuqsan aur mukhalifat aaye, tab bhi imaan ko haath se na jaane dena asal kaamyabi hai.

5.  **Surah Luqman (Priority 5): Mohsineen ki Sifaat aur Aulad ki Tarbiyat**
    *   Insaan khud namaz, zakat, aakhirat ka yaqeen aur ehsaan wali zindagi ikhtiyar kare aur apni aulad ki tarbiyat kare. Aaj ke materialistic daur mein parents hi wahid kadi hain jo apni naslon ke imaan ki hifazat kar sakte hain. Yehi Quran classes ka main implementation hai.

### Chhati (6th) Aakhirat ki Tarjeeh: Surah Zariyat – Qaumon ki Tabahi ke Usool aur Nijaat

**Fikri Tarjeeh Likhiye:**
"Qaumon ki tabahi ke usool samjha kar ghaflat chhorna, Allah ki taraf bhaagna aur bandagi ko zindagi ka markaz banana."

**Pehla Pehlu: Qaumon ki Androoni Tabahi ki Teen Wajoohat (Bimaariyan)**

Quraan pehle woh muqaam dikhata hai jahan tabahi ki beej insaan ke andar padta hai. Qaumein achanak nahi girtin, pehle andar se khokhli hoti hain.

1.  **"Kharrasoon" (گمان / Guman):** Yaqeen ko chhod kar gumaan par jeenay walay. Aakhirat, hisaab aur wahi ki haqeeqat ko yaqeen se qubool karne ke bajaye apni khwahishat, mahol aur apni logic ke mutabiq adjust karna. Decision making se aakhirat ka wazan nikal jana.

2.  **"Fi Ghamratin" (غمرة / Doob):** Haqeeqat ko dhaanp dene wali gehri doob. Yeh **Modern Ignorance** hai. Insaan duniya ke system, career aur planning mein expert hai lekin Allah ke saamne khade hone ki tayyari uski zindagi ka centre point nahi. Insaan doob chuka hai magar use mehsoos nahi hota.

3.  **"Sahoon" (ساہون / Ghaflat):** Haq ke samne aane ke bawajood be-fikr rehna. Woh sunta hai, maanta hai, discuss karta hai magar badalta nahi aur seriously nahi leta. Aahista aahista deen ka wazan aur priority khatam ho jati hai.

**Dusra Pehlu: Tabahi se Bachne ka Amali Nizam**

1.  **Raath ki Tanhai aur Maal ka Rukh:** Raat ko thoda sote hain, sehr ke waqt istighfar karte hain, aur apne maal mein saail (maangne wale) aur mehroom (zarooratmand magar na maangne wale) ka haq rakhte hain. (*"Kanu Qaleelam min al-Laili ma yahja'oon. Wa bil as-haar hum yastaghfiroon. Wa fi amwaalihim haqqun lis-sa'ili wal-mahroom."*)

2.  **Ghaur wa Fikr:** Zameen aur aasmaan ki nishaniyon mein ghaur karna, risq ke system ko dekhna. (*"Wa fil-ardi aayaatul lil-mooqineen. Wa fi anfusikum afala tubsiroon."*)

3.  **Allah ki Taraf Daudna:** (*"Fafirroo ila Allah"*) – Allah ke raste mein jiddo-jehed karo. Bandagi ko maqsad-e-zindagi bana lo. (*"Wa ma khalaqtul jinna wal insa illa li ya'budoon."*)

**Checklist (Evaluation):**
Kya main yaqeen chhod kar gumaan mein jee raha hoon? Kya main jadeed jahalat aur duniya ki gehrai mein doob raha hoon? Kya main jaanne ke bawajood ghaflat mein pada hoon? Kya meri raat, mera istighfar, mera maal aakhirat ke rukh mein lag raha hai?

### Saatwi (7th) Aakhirat ki Tarjeeh: Surah Ambiya – Ambiya ke Ausaaf aur Maqsad wali Zindagi

"Ambiya ki ausaaf se maqsad wali zindagi aur aakhirat ki kaamyabi."

Allah Ta'ala ne Ambiya ka zikr sirf isliye nahi kiya ke humein naam maaloom ho jayein, balki isliye ke hum dekhein ke aakhirat mein kaamyaab hone wali shakhsiyat duniya mein kaise jee kar gayi. Yeh log sirf musalle ke aabid nahi thay, balki maidan-e-amal mein haq ke liye khade hone wale thay. Quran ne maidan ki jiddo-jehad ko highlight kiya.

*   **Musa aur Haroon (AS):** Fir'auni nizaam ke samne Furqan ki roshni lekar khade hone wali leadership.
*   **Ibrahim (AS):** Akele mahol mein bhi haq ke liye qurbani ka kirdar. (Hajj unki jiddo-jehad ki naqal hai).
*   **Loot (AS):** Behaya muashare ke andar pakizgi aur ikhlaqi hadood par qayam rehne wale daayi.
*   **Nooh (AS):** Taweel dawat, mussalsal sabr aur nataaij ko Allah ke hawale karne wale daayi.
*   **Dawood aur Sulaiman (AS):** Ilm, quwwat, leadership ko Allah ke hukm ke taabe rakhne wale hukmran.
*   **Ayyub (AS):** Dard, bimari, tootne wale haalaat mein bhi adab aur rehmat-e-Ilahi par yaqeen.
*   **Ismail, Idrees, Zulkifl (AS):** Sabr par qayam rehne wali shakhsiyat. (*"Kullum minas-Sabireen"*)
*   **Yunus (AS):** Ghalati ke baad zid nahi, tareekiyon (andheron) mein bhi tauba.
*   **Zakariya aur Yahya (AS):** Kamzor asbaab ke bawajood bhi dua aur umeed.
*   **Maryam (AS):** Pakizgi, haya aur Allah par aitmaad se duniya ke liye nishani.

**Ambiya ki Teen Common Khoobiyaan:**
1.  *"Kaanoo yusari'oona fil khairaat"* – Naiki ke kaamon mein daud lagane wale (Mission fit).
2.  *"Yad'oonana raghaban wa rahaba"* – Umeed aur khauf ke saath Allah ko pukarna.
3.  *"Wa kaanoo lana khashi'een"* – Allah ke saamne aajizi aur khushu wale.

### Aathwi (8th) Aakhirat ki Tarjeeh: Surah Ankabut – Aazmaish mein Imaan ki Sachchai

"Aazmaishon mein imaan ko sachcha saabit karna, deen ke raste ki jiddo-jehad ko priority dena."

1.  **Imaan ka Dawa aur Aazmaish:** Imaan ka dawa aazmaish ke baghair sachcha saabit nahi hota. (*"Ahasiban naasu an yutrakoo an yaqoolo aamanna wa hum la yuftanoon."*)
2.  **Jiddo-jehad aur Nijaat:** Deen ke raste ki jiddo-jehad bande ko apni nijaat ka rasta dikhati hai. (*"Wa man jaahada fainnama yujaahidu li nafsihi. InnAllaha la ghaniyyun anil aalameen."*) Insaan duniya ke career aur comfort ke liye kitni mehnat karta hai, lekin deen ke liye priority nahi deta.
3.  **Duniya ki Haqeeqat:** Dunya ki zindagi sirf khel tamasha hai. (*"Wa ma hazihil hayatud dunya illa lahwun wa la'ab."*)

---

### Nauwi (9th) Aakhirat ki Tarjeeh: Surah Kahf – Fiton ke Daur mein Imaan ki Hifazat

"Fiton ke daur mein imaan ki hifazat, ilm-o-adab aur taaqat ko amanat samajh kar aakhirat ke taabe karna."

1.  **Ashaab-e-Kahf:** Fiton (Dajjal ke fitne) ke daur mein imaan ki hifazat. Allah se taluq, dil ki mazbooti, aur **saleh sohbat** zaroori hai. (*"Wa asbir nafsaka ma'allazeena yad'oona rabbahum..."*) – Apne aap ko un logon ke saath jamaye rakho jo subah shaam apne rab ko pukarte hain.
2.  **Musa aur Khizr (AS):** Ilm ke raaste mein sabr, aajizi aur ustaad ki rehnumai ka sabq. Ilm ke liye adab zaroori hai, har baat par fauran sawal nahi karna chahiye balki observe karna seekhiye.
3.  **Zulqarnain:** Taaqat ke bawajood ita'at, adal aur amanat. Taaqat ko apni jagir nahi balki Allah ki ata samajhna aur logon ke nafa ke liye istemal karna. (*"Haaza rahmatum mir-Rabbi"*)

---

### Daswi (10th) Aakhirat ki Tarjeeh: Surah Zumar – Deen ka Ikhlass aur Qafila

"Jis ki priority aakhirat hoti hai, uska deen khalis hota hai, umeed zinda hoti hai, qafila sahih hota hai."

1.  **Deen ko Khalis Karna:** Deen ko Allah ke liye pure (khalis) kar lo. Do kashti ke sawaar mat bano, apne aap ko purely Allah ke liye devoted karo. (*"Faa'budillaaha mukhliseena lahud-deen."*)
2.  **Sohbat aur Qafila:** Aakhirat ka qafila duniya ki sohbat aur fikr se banta hai. Qayamat ke din tum unhi ke saath uthaye jaoge jin ke saath duniya mein rahe. (*"Wa seeqal lazeena kafaroo ila jahannama zumara." / "Wa seeqal lazeena ttaqaw rabbahum ilal jannati zumara."*) Apni sohbat, mahol, dosti aur attachments par ghaur karo. Agar ghaflat ka qafila hai, toh anjam khatarnaak hai.

### Conclusion aur Amali Qadam
Doston, paanch nabvi ka poora safar yahan khatam hua. Quran ne humein sirf maloomaat nahi di, balki poori zindagi ka simt badalne wali dawat di hai. Yeh 10 aakhirat ki tarjeehat sirf sun lene, note kar lene ya yaad kar lene ke liye nahi hain, balki apni zindagi ke faislon aur aadaton mein tabdeeli laane ke liye hain.

Humaari Quran classes, physical halqe, nashistein, naqeeb ke saath mulaqaat, phone calls – yeh sab sirf intezami cheezein nahi hain, balki aakhirat ki tarjeehat ko zindagi mein laane ka zariya hain. Apne naqeebon se raabta karein, rasman nahi balki zarooratan aur talab samajh kar.

Faisla aapko karna hai: Kya aap "Fafirroo ila Allah" wale banna chahte hain ya khali sun kar apni purani zindagi mein hi rehna chahte hain?

Allah aapko aur mujhe taufeeq ata farmaye sahih andaaz mein Quran ko seekhne, samajhne aur amal karne ka. Jazakum Allahu Khairan.
